Spencer Applegate

About Me

Hi, my name is Spencer Applegate. I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Cincinnati. I am currently working toward both my bachelor's and master's degrees through UC's accelerated engineering program. I have always been interested in figuring out how things work and finding ways to improve them.

Engineering & Research

A lot of my time is spent working on engineering projects and research. I am especially interested in robotics, materials science, and product design. My current research involves magnetically responsive soft materials, which can bend and move when they are exposed to a magnetic field. I like engineering because it gives me the chance to actually build and test ideas instead of only learning about them in a classroom.

Startups & Business

Outside of engineering, I am also interested in startups and entrepreneurship. I am involved with Bearcat Ventures, UC's student-run venture capital fund, where I get to learn about new companies and technologies. I enjoy talking with founders, learning how businesses work, and seeing how an idea can eventually become a real product or company.

Outside of School

When I am not working on school, research, or other projects, I like learning about new technology and coming up with ideas of my own. Some of the areas I find most interesting are robotics, artificial intelligence, space, and emerging technology. I also enjoy spending time with friends and trying new things. Overall, I would describe myself as someone who is curious & always looking for something new to learn.
